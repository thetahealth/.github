# Theta Health

**Before doctors, beyond records.**

Theta Health is an AI-powered health data platform that brings your wearable data, medical records (EHR/FHIR), and lab reports together into one unified system — with AI-driven interpretation that helps you understand what your body is telling you.

🌐 [thetahealth.ai](https://www.thetahealth.ai/)

## Mirobody

[**Mirobody**](https://github.com/thetahealth/mirobody) is Theta Health's open-source project, and the core technology behind **Theta Wellness**.
